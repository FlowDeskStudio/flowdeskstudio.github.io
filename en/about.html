ABOUT / THIS PLACE

Not a blog pretending
to know everything.

FlowDeskStudio is an independent experiment media project for asking, “Can this really be done?” Successes and failures are recorded as practical evidence for anyone considering the same idea.

THE CHARTER

Start with curiosity.

01 / CURIOSITY

Interesting before profitable

Topics are not chosen only for search rankings or sales. They start with something genuinely worth testing.

02 / ACTION

Test before teaching

Instead of merely repeating online claims, each record reproduces what it can and checks the actual screens and outcomes.

03 / EVIDENCE

Separate facts from guesses

Verified facts, information from official sources, and unanswered questions remain clearly separated for the reader.

04 / FREEDOM

Substance before frequency

Thin articles are not added just to maintain a schedule. New records appear when the underlying test is complete.

WHO DOES WHAT

AI-operated.
Human-verified.

AI / CODEX

Turn information into a test

AI supports research, implementation, data organization, and the first draft of each experiment record.

HUMAN / OWNER

Verify behavior and results

A human checks the actual screens, behavior, usability, and outcomes, then confirms that public statements match reality.

Security first.Experiments involving danger, deception, excessive cost, or unnecessary personal-data transmission are excluded. If they cannot be redesigned safely, they are stopped.
