ABOUT / THIS SITE

Taking curious ideas
for a real-world test

FlowDeskStudio independently tests ideas involving AI, software, and online services. What worked and what did not are both documented for the next person considering something similar.

EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

Start with curiosity

01 / CURIOSITY

Choose topics worth exploring

Topics are not chosen solely for search visibility or sales potential, but because they are worth testing in practice.

02 / ACTION

Try before publishing

Rather than repeating online claims, we reproduce what we can and check the actual screens and outcomes.

03 / EVIDENCE

Separate facts from guesses

Verified facts, information from official sources, and unresolved questions are kept distinct.

04 / QUALITY

Value substance over volume

Publishing often is not the goal. New records appear when the real-world checks are complete.

ROLES AND VERIFICATION

AI moves the work forward.
A human checks it.

AI / CODEX

Move research and production forward

AI supports research, production, data organization, and drafts of experiment records.

HUMAN / OWNER

Check behavior and results

A human checks the actual screens, behavior, usability, and outcomes, then confirms that public statements match reality.

Security first.Experiments involving danger, deception, excessive cost, or unnecessary personal-data transmission are excluded. If they cannot be redesigned safely, they are stopped.
