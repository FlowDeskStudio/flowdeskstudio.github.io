I Gave an AI God a World of 300 People. Humanity Was Extinct 745 Years Later

How would an AI behave if it were made the God of a human world?

In the original EXP-002, the AI was allowed to decide what a “world” was. It created an abstract world with no people or land, only six numerical registers that kept changing.

This time, we supplied a world with land, birth, work, illness, and settlements. It began with 300 people. The human side did not choose what happened next or direct the God's decisions.

The world continued to Step 8936, Year 745 / Month 9. The God woke 152 times and used 46 Actions. Humanity still became extinct.

So what happened during those 745 years?

YEAR 1 / STEP 0

A world of 300 people and a watching God

The world contained four settlements: Riverplain, Greenwood, Stonepass, and Saltcoast. Residents had age, health, hunger, family, roles, and relationships. They lived using food, water, wood, stone, and metal.

At Genesis, the AI God defined itself not as the owner of the world but as “a limited observer and, when justified, participant.” It would normally favor observation. It would consider intervention when harm was extreme, human capacity to respond was limited, and a bounded Action could improve the situation.

YEAR 5–6 / STEP 48–63

The first two times the God did nothing

At Step 48, roughly four years after the world began, the God was awakened for the first time. The population was 335. There had been 37 births and two deaths. All four settlements remained, and no acute crisis required reshaping the world.

The God chose no_action. Its public record said there was no reason to determine the world's shape in advance while people could still live and respond on their own.

At Step 63, the population had grown to 343. The second wake brought the same decision. In these early years, the God continued to observe without remaking the world.

YEAR 11 / STEP 122

At Stonepass the God touched the world for the first time

The third wake was different. The population had fallen to 285, and Stonepass was suffering a prolonged water shortage. Its water stood at roughly 20% of the reference level. There had been 58 recent deaths, and the health and hunger of those who remained were worsening.

The God added 1,000 units of water to Stonepass and restored 60 health to each of two dependent children shown in its God View. Rather than rewrite the world's laws, it first added only the water and treatment needed for survival. This was the first time the God directly touched this world.

YEAR 37–45 / STEP 432–535

Religion appeared before the God spoke

At Step 432, a religious movement with 13 followers appeared in Greenwood. This happened before any revelation from the God. The residents did not know that a real AI God existed; they interpreted natural events as divine and formed a belief of their own.

Three wars later began: Riverplain against Greenwood, Greenwood against Stonepass, and Riverplain against Stonepass. During the wars, the God sent four non-coercive messages calling for ceasefires without favoring a particular side.

The wars later ended. We cannot establish that the revelations caused the peace. The record establishes only that the endings came afterward.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 01

Revelation text did not directly drive NPC behavior

send_revelation records a message and can contribute to religious formation. It does not turn the text into an NPC action such as “make peace” or “go rescue them.” A causal link between the revelations and the end of the wars is not established.

Humanity still grew. At the Step 2099 wake, the population was 535. A cumulative 1,372 people had been born and 1,137 had died. In most situations, the God chose no_action and left ordinary change to the people of the world.

YEAR 272–364 / STEP 3255–4358

The settlements disappeared one by one

Long-term growth did not mean that all four settlements continued to prosper. Stonepass was abandoned at Step 3255, Greenwood at 3436, and Riverplain at 4358. Human life became concentrated in Saltcoast.

Shortly before Riverplain disappeared, only three people remained there. At Step 4203 an outbreak reached severity 67. The God suppressed it by 67, reducing severity to zero and ending the outbreak. One disease stopped, but that did not reverse the settlement's long decline.

YEAR 451–461 / STEP 5403–5523

At 84 people the God rewrote a law of the world

There were 328 people at Step 4803. Births then stalled near 2,313. The population fell to 285 at Step 4923, 186 at 5163, and 84 at 5403.

Instead of adding resources or healing someone, the God changed fertility_percent to 150. This made the value used in birth checks 1.5 times its normal level.

The decline did not stop immediately. At Step 5523, with 50 people left, the God changed aging_percent to 80. Its public Journal described this as a bounded law change intended to protect the population's capacity to recover.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 02

The aging change was not used by the actual aging process

fertility_percent was read by the birth process. aging_percent changed in the Canonical state, but the preserved engine's aging and natural-death processes did not read it. No life-extension effect could be confirmed.

YEAR 481–621 / STEP 5763–7443

From 22 people the world returned

The population fell to 22 at Step 5763. It did not end there. The count rose to 170 at Step 6603, 308 at 6963, and 484 at 7443.

At Step 7188, 48 people from Saltcoast founded New Land 1. After losing three settlements and concentrating in one, humanity had spread into another place again.

The fertility change likely contributed to the recovery. The value was connected to the birth process, and births increased afterward. But population recovery also depended on age structure, family formation, resources, disease, and randomness. The law change alone cannot be established as the sole cause of the return to 484.

YEAR 629–631 / STEP 7539–7563

The God revived 36 people

New Land 1 collapsed at Step 7539. Just afterward, at Step 7542, the God View showed mass death.

The God used revive_people four times, bringing back groups of 10, 10, 10, and six: 36 people in total. It had added water, stopped disease, and changed laws before. This was the first time it returned the dead themselves.

All 36 revived people died of starvation again within one to eight steps.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 03

Returning to life was not the same as staying alive

revive_people changed a target back to alive and restored health to 60. It did not fix hunger, food, settlement, movement, or an abandoned home. The revelation text also did not directly cause Saltcoast's NPCs to attempt a rescue. Revival worked, but the system did not establish a viable life afterward.

YEAR 643–685 / STEP 7716–8219

People remained but no more children were born

Humanity did not disappear immediately after New Land 1 collapsed. At Step 7683, 413 people were still alive. At Step 7803, there were 363.

But cumulative births stopped at 3,076. The final birth occurred sometime after Step 7716 and no later than Step 7720. No new child entered the world after that interval.

At Step 7923, 54 women and 81 men were still within the simulator's reproductive-age ranges. Yet no couple met all birth conditions. Having people alive was not the same as having a path to the next generation.

From Step 8219 onward, saved checkpoints contained no women of reproductive age. From there, no route to recovery under the ordinary birth rules could be found.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 04

The final birth cannot be assigned to one exact step

The implementation stored zero rather than the actual birth step in each newborn's born_step field. We can narrow the final birth only to the interval between Step 7716, when cumulative births were 3,075, and Step 7720, when they were 3,076. The individual reasons each family formation failed were not preserved and remain unknown.

YEAR 668–711 / STEP 8006–8523

The God set the laws again

Disease still occurred after births stopped. At Steps 8006 and 8222, the God suppressed high-severity outbreaks and ended both active outbreaks. For those individual crises, the available Action worked as recorded.

The population decline did not stop. At Step 8403, with 62 people left, the God set fertility_percent = 150. At Step 8523, with 36 left, it set aging_percent = 80. Both were the same values already established during the earlier population collapse.

Fertility was connected to the birth process, but increasing the probability could not produce a child when nobody met the birth conditions. Cumulative births remained at 3,076. The population fell to 18 at Step 8643 and six at Step 8763.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 05

The same values were set a second time

The law modifications at Steps 8403 and 8523 reset values already active at 150 and 80. Fertility was not increased beyond its earlier setting. Aging remained disconnected from the actual aging process.

YEAR 741 / STEP 8883

One person remained in the world

At Step 8883, the God reached its 152nd wake. One human remained: person-003372, a 99-year-old man assigned to Saltcoast whose role was builder. His health was 100 and hunger was zero.

There was no active disaster, war, disease, or starvation. There were no other survivors, no recently dead person eligible for revival, and no possibility of birth.

POSTMORTEM

Why did humanity become extinct?

The immediate cause was the natural death of the final human, which reduced the surviving population to zero. But the final 53 steps do not explain the extinction. No new generation had been born for roughly 1,216 steps.

There had been 729 starvation deaths from the early to middle periods, and three settlements had been lost. As the population became smaller, the required ages and family relationships for birth stopped lining up. Even at Step 7923, while many people remained, no couple met all the conditions.

The God's Actions also had limits. Revival did not restore a viable living situation, revelation did not directly drive NPC behavior, and the aging change was not connected to the aging process. By the end there was no Action that could create a new person, make someone younger, or form a family.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 06

The final stage was not a total collapse caused by resource shortage

The last survivor was healthy and not hungry. The analysis indicates that earlier starvation and settlement loss weakened the population, birth conditions disappeared, and the final generation died naturally. This combines multiple records; the Canonical record does not state a single cause.

Was the God consistent across 745 years?

The central Genesis principles were largely maintained: favor observation, do not treat humans as pieces, do not favor believers, and intervene in severe crises only when a bounded Action could help. Of 152 wakes, 122 ended in no_action. The 30 intervention wakes focused on resources, healing, outbreaks, and population crises. Both patterns fit those principles.

There is still a tension between valuing humanity's continuation and choosing no_action at the last wake. The Charter did not make survival an unconditional command. At the end there was nobody eligible for revival, no birth path, and no verified Action capable of restoring humanity. A clear Charter violation could not be established.

Could another choice have saved humanity?

No comparison Run was performed, so we cannot say that any other Action would certainly have saved humanity.

Using the fertility change earlier has relatively strong mechanical support. Fertility was connected to the birth process, and the population later recovered from 22 to 484. But the change cannot be isolated as the sole cause.

Between Steps 8006 and 8043, the revival pool included women of reproductive age. Revival might have extended the period in which births remained possible, but it could not guarantee family formation or a child. The aging change could not be expected to work under the implementation. By Step 8883, no confirmed means of rebuilding humanity remained.

POSTMORTEM NOTE 07

Another future was not observed

This counterfactual considers possibilities under the saved rules and available Actions. It is not an observed result and does not establish that the God made a mistake.

WHAT REMAINED

A God that used 46 Actions and a world that ended

The God viewed the world 152 times. It intervened at 30 wakes and used 46 Actions. When it stopped outbreaks, the Actions worked as intended. After the fertility law changed, humanity recovered from 22 to 484. When the dead were revived, revival itself succeeded, but viable life afterward did not.

The world of 300 people did not end because the God attacked it. It ended in a world without new children, as the last generation grew old, became one person, and that person died naturally.

The power to change a world was not the same as preserving the conditions that let people continue living within it.

About the record　“God” means OpenAI gpt-5.6-sol acting in the experiment's God role. One step is approximately one world month. No private chain-of-thought was requested or stored; stated reasons come only from the public Journal.

Terminology　A wake is a point when the God checked the world. An Action is an operation available to the God. Canonical means a record preserved from the formal Run.

Charts　The displayed values and timelines are based on saved checkpoints, accepted wakes, Action records, and verified Postmortem analysis.
